Correction to historical design metadata

  The 3,320 saved whole-archive design.json files are historical records and were not

rewritten. Their field annual model states that “held region and connected groups ex-

cluded.” That statement is correct for the held test connected groups, but it is not

an unconditional region exclusion. The original forward HUC17 estimator retained

one other same-region training group, and NVE interval calibration retained two other

same-region groups. The paper therefore distinguishes the held-test-group main anal-

data; it does not change any saved prediction. A correction manifest is available in

S2.   Observation-rule accounting

  The rule table uses every eligible station-year, not only the selected witnesses. A reported

window is not a complete-year H30, and an accepted incomplete year can contain an

unobserved higher peak. The “new facts” column therefore compares reported windows

with the maximum reported window among the other accepted years; it does not prove

a full-record ordering. The measurement-margin sensitivity treats a window ordering as

unresolved when the reported difference is at most twice the assumed absolute error on

each window. These margins are assumptions, not sensor-error estimates.


S3.   Joint-rank numerical convergence

  This is a fixed-marginal numerical-budget check, not the source-role experiment. It

keeps the saved annual quantiles, observed bounds and evaluation years and increases only

the stratified sample count through 256, 1,024 and 4,096 on the difficult forward controlled

gaps. Three random seeds index the rank construction. The rank sequence is generated at

4,096 draws and truncated for smaller counts; annual marginals are regenerated separately

for each sample size and remain specific to each estimator. Coverage, width and interval

score are reported with the across-seed range. The range is not a confidence interval, and

the check uses the same opened data as the main analysis. A 0.3-point coverage rule

and 1% width and interval-score rules are applied to the three-seed mean. Five of six

method–construction pairs meet the coverage rule on that mean. The largest three-seed-

mean coverage change is 0.36 percentage points, and the largest single-seed change is 0.815

percentage points. Averaging seeds can cancel opposing changes, so these rules are not

a resolution guarantee and the constructions are not ranked. At 4,096 samples, nominal

  For ensemble x1 , . . . , xM and outcome y, the continuous ranked probability score is

Threshold-weighted scoring replaces samples and outcome by their maxima with the

training H30 threshold. The 90% interval score adds twenty times the distance outside

the interval to its width. A 95% trend interval score uses the corresponding factor forty.

Missing predictions remain unavailable rather than being assigned zero error.

  Both annual estimators use the observed-window support projection where a finite

bound exists. The projection is applied to the annual distribution and then carried

into the slope; a trend interval is not projected onto an annual lower bound. Seven

stored quantiles do not determine their outer tails; the normal-spacing continuation and

the alternative constant tail are assumptions. Within one estimator, cross-year rank

constructions keep that estimator’s annual marginal samples fixed. The two estimators

have different annual marginals. The constructions are not a posterior for the fitted annual

model. The source-only corrections and the post-hoc propagation reported below do not

establish a finite-sample or independent-climate coverage guarantee.

S8.   Expanded missingness-mechanism sensitivities

  After the earlier results and seven-site pilot had been inspected, we fixed an expansion

to the first five masks for all 41 forward-eligible original archive stations in 17 connected

groups. Only controlled gaps and one seed-zero source-model assignment are studied. For

the donor sensitivity, the target masks are unchanged and those same dates are hidden

in every other panel column before local fitting. All 205 settings have at least one finite

donor-panel observation removed; the total of 427,375 finite donor-panel cells counts

repeated masks and may include columns not ultimately selected by the local estimator.

It is not a count of independent outages or necessarily of observations the estimator would

have used.

  For the heat-associated sensitivity, the original 35% Bernoulli draw determines the

S35.1. Common support and common-weight standardization

  Common geometric cells contain gap length, training-peak overlap, year and visible-

donor bins. Screening to shared cells preserves region-specific cell frequencies and is not

balancing. The primary standardized target assigns identical weights proportional to

the smaller regional cell count; equal-cell weighting is a sensitivity. Joint-cell frequency

TV becomes zero under either common target, but within-bin continuous quantities and

training histories remain imbalanced. In the geometry min-count analysis, mean training

complete-year counts are 9.78 in FOEN versus 22.96 in Bavaria (SMD -3.052); after

report continuous means, weighted KS distances and missing-weight fractions. A coverage

contrast remains under these common geometric-cell weights; residual within-cell and

history differences remain, and the comparison does not identify a reconstructor effect or

exclude river processes. The absence of a Swiss air-ridge stratum prevents corresponding

cross-region comparison of the main Bavarian failure layer.

S36.4. Same-day missingness with an evaluable-peer denominator

  Peer-evaluable rows require another station in the same connected group with an eligible

recorded missingness pattern in that year. Observed peers use the eligible-year roster,

which includes incomplete years; trial peers use the complete-reference-year roster on

which masks are imposed. Group-years with no such peer are excluded and counted.

Same-day overlap means at least one missing day coincides with a peer missing day. Shared

station-days count each focal station’s missing days that coincide with any peer, divided

by its eligible missing station-days. Observed rows are incomplete station-years; trial rows

are station-year–replicates. Same-day co-missingness does not identify an outage cause,

the donor actually used, or a naturally unobserved failure process.

S38.      Post-hoc retained-truth and point-summary checks

  These checks reuse the saved forward and offline predictions. They do not refit models,

open new stations, or supply an independent validation. Replacing a retained annual value

by the experimental truth cannot be done when that year is actually missing.

  On each common station–repetition, the warm-season year set is held fixed and every

retained D2 annual value is replaced by the known complete-record H30 before the slope

is recomputed. The resulting root-mean-square error is the error of the year set alone.

Table S70 shows that this truth-in-set error is nearly the same as the error of the saved

D2 values, especially for forward controlled gaps (0.445242 versus 0.445331 ◦ C per decade).

For a finite complete record, the warm-season screen changes the slope mainly by changing

which years and ordinary-least-squares weights remain. Making the retained years exact

does not restore the years that were removed.

  The two root-mean-square errors are not subtracted, and neither is divided by the

other to name a percentage caused by retention. The squared error of the warm-season

slope relative to the complete-record slope also contains the cross term between the year-

set change and the reconstruction error on the retained years. The four-cell symmetric

decomposition in the main text keeps its original definition. The target remains the slope

of a particular complete archive, not a latent climate-process trend.

  The main analysis publishes the annual median and then scores the trend of that

published series. A conditional mean is the relevant point for squared error of a slope

when the conditional distribution is correct; the median does not automatically have

that property. Table S71 replaces only the annual point, using the mean of the same

approximate distribution on the 738 archives where both methods have a forward controlled-

the normal-spacing tails. Both root-mean-square errors change slightly, and the original

S39.    Independent observed-record arithmetic re-audits

  Using the same local canonical float32 daily archive and an independent convolution

implementation, we recomputed 2,139 complete reference station-years and 545 incomplete

station-years at 96 eligible stations. All 35 witness identities, saved 30-day values and

complete-year reference maxima agreed within the stated numerical tolerance. This

is an arithmetic re-audit of the same processed archive, not an independent check of

provider-native records or sensor error.

  Comparing 971,673 finite values from 34 selected project input panels with the canonical

archive yielded no missingness or float32-cast discrepancies. The largest absolute rounding

difference is 0.00000153 degrees C; 0 witness identities and 0 classifications at the 0.5-degree

excess threshold changed. The input panels are processed project files, not provider-native

instrument records.

  Across 2,700 eligible station-years, independently recalculated annual coverage, available

30-day maxima, July–August completeness and longest missing runs reproduced 210

witness–rule decisions. For each of 35 witness years, increasing its observed values by 5

degrees C did not change the leave-target-year-out warm-season climatology. The old

fixed-season fraction remains sourced from saved metadata; these probes do not cover

every year or other pipelines.

  For the ten additional USGS sites, independent parsing of the saved daily API responses

reproduced 8 approved-complete reference station-years and the 9 certified observed

windows. The original quality-screened window selection also replays from the saved

payloads, but the separate arithmetic audit does not independently implement that

selection. Approved estimated values remain in complete-year references, but the certified
