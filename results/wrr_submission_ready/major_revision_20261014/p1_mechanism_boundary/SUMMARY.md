## Falsification statements

- This analysis fails to support a reconstruction-independent geometry law if the position contrast does not survive with the air reconstructor, if the length effect at a fixed hidden share does not exclude zero for the reconstructors the manuscript leans on, or if the misalignment share is near zero.
- The design-based phase interval refutes the fixed A/sigma threshold if a substantial share of cases retained by that threshold has a design-based interval wider than the study's own phase tolerance; it supports the threshold if the two mostly agree.
- Competing-peak and residual-correlation conditions are observational properties of the cases; a null there falsifies the mechanism reading, not the panel.

## What this does not prove

- It does not prove that misalignment, competing peaks or residual autocorrelation *cause* the error; they are measured on the same cases, not randomised.
- The competing-peak gap C is a station-year property, so its error relationship is confounded with station identity and cannot be separated from it here.
- The air reconstructor is fitted to a reanalysis forcing (NASA POWER v18 table); it is not an in-situ air measurement, and its contrasts are properties of that product.
- The phase interval is computed on the observed-day fit (the protocol the manuscript calls the authors' own, unrepaired). It is not a repaired-series interval, and it is conditional on an AR(1)/Bartlett model for the residual correlation rather than on the full residual covariance.
- Nothing here is a pre-registered confirmation; no number may move a headline claim without the Step-2/Step-3 gates.
