# Scientific revision 20261020c

The experiments are complete scientific tests, not a claim that all hypotheses
succeeded. Read the [Chinese assessment](../../../docs/reviews/wrr_major_revision_20261020c/EXECUTION_REPORT_ZH.md)
and [current paper](../../../paper/wrr/README.md).

- r3_01--r3_02: review evidence, public mountain resource, metadata-only roles and continuous agency acquisition.
- r3_03/r3_05: broad paired mechanisms, documented-mean sensitivity, exact switch identity, factorial oracle and small deployable correction.
- r3_04/admission and r3_07/admission: final source admission comparisons and original-pool decisions. Older controlled-classifier results outside these admission subdirectories are superseded diagnostics.
- r3_06: all-old-target/donor identity audit and clustered risk counterexamples.
- r3_08/r3_09/r3_10: separate development/calibration/test cases, frozen predictive objects and independent operating/utility outcomes.
- r3_11/r3_12: CSDI and Air2stream references, with availability and matched subsets.
- r3_13/admission: 1000 full learner/calibration refits per protocol, repeated learning curves and qualified power projections.
- r3_14/r3_15: realistic admission generation and nondegenerate held-source event/calendar cofailure assays.
- r3_16: frozen source composition model and independent calendar checks.
- r3_17/r3_18/r3_20: numerical claim bindings, evidence figures and generated SI table sources.
- r3_19: corrected descriptive hydrological labels; immutable predictive decisions are unchanged.
- r3_22: scientific requirements, environment, document and validation audit.

The protocol, method and deployment locks have different roles. None is
overwritten after a poor test outcome. A local historical reconstruction fit is
allowed; hidden target-year labels do not choose deployment methods. Seven
Swiss documented-mean test components and twelve French aggregate components
are not nineteen equivalent primary replications.

The uncertain-window practical superiority claim is not established. Unchanged
transfer exceeds its target at observed operating points. A small-component
interval does not prove population risk exceeds five percent either; no
transferable risk certificate is claimed. Declared-cost downstream benefits do
not imply free recovery, unbiased regional samples or superiority over every
physical reconstruction workflow.

Old raw hydrological labels used a snow-percentage unit incorrectly; corrected
tables and the dated erratum supersede only that descriptive label. Raw outputs,
fitted objects, thresholds and role locks are preserved.

No author metadata or permanent analysis archive is invented. Raw data remain
subject to provider redistribution rights. No journal submission is made.

The Git package contains the frozen deployment heads, CSDI reference checkpoint,
locks, acquisition manifests, numerical summaries, figure/table sources and
validation logs. Provider downloads, daily source bundles, large controlled and
monitoring case caches, cross-fit model/score caches and training scratch are
not redistributed. The duplicate `r3_17_evidence/independent_decisions.csv` is
also omitted; its canonical decision-cost outputs remain in
`r3_10_external_decisions/`. The preserved custody chain is required for the
full frozen replay; a Git checkout is not the permanent analysis archive.
