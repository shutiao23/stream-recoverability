Selected daily-temperature archive for observation-only witness verification

This is a project-processed, float32, quality-filtered subset of provider data,
not the original provider-native measurements or a statement of sensor accuracy.
Data and derived outputs are limited to previously selected eligible stations
with documented source/derivative reuse. The study's one LUBW eligible station
is excluded pending permission. Selection, aggregation, quality filtering,
float32 conversion, and the derived station-year/window calculations are
project changes; dates, available values, and station identities are retained.

USGS: U.S. Geological Survey, Water Data for the Nation, daily water temperature,
https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/; acknowledge the U.S. Geological Survey.

GKD Bayern: Datenquelle: Bayerisches Landesamt fuer Umwelt, www.lfu.bayern.de;
water temperature, https://www.gkd.bayern.de/de/fluesse/wassertemperatur/tabellen;

CHMI: Czech Hydrometeorological Institute, historical measured water temperature,
https://opendata.chmi.cz/hydrology/historical/data/measured_temperature/ ;
CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .

FOEN: Federal Office for the Environment (FOEN), Hydrological observations,
https://api.data-platform.cloud.bafu.admin.ch/en/dataproduct-water-observations ;
Open use. Must provide the source; https://api.data-platform.cloud.bafu.admin.ch/en/lizenz-und-quelle .

The candidate is a local review artifact, not a permanent public archive.
