# Route A second-confirmation protocol

The executable contract is
`configs/route_a_second_confirmation_protocol.yaml`. This protocol replaces
the former cliff-edge sample rule with an attrition-tolerant primary analysis:
the achieved independent-network sample is analyzed when at least 40 networks
arrive, while 60--80 scored networks remain the power target. Candidate
recruitment starts at 150 because observed daily-value retention is close to
one half.

The first 42 scored Route A networks are permanently ineligible for this
second confirmation. Analyses that use their labels to estimate a domain
recalibration or risk-control budget are explicitly post-confirmation method
development. They cannot become evidence for the second confirmation.

The primary inferential unit is a river network. Station-gap rows are nested
measurements. C1 reports network rank before pooled rank; C2 compares the
simple model with a fitting-period empirical-loss curve; C3 evaluates a
network-grouped learn-then-test triage rule and real-data station-retention
replay; C4 reports every sufficiently supported provider domain and the
predeclared thermal-state screen.

This file is a protocol, not proof of a scored result. Recruitment now contains
242 second-confirmation candidates and 60 strict-QC arrivals: 35 US, 15 Czech,
and 10 Norwegian networks. The candidate, total-arrival, US, and two-European-
domain floors pass. The Canadian floor does not. An official four-station
Canadian Coast Guard source supplied 16,244 daily observations but explicitly
labels them as not validated or checked, so strict QC retained zero stations.
Second-confirmation scoring remains withheld until a validated Canadian river
network arrives; the Canadian requirement is not dropped after source review.
