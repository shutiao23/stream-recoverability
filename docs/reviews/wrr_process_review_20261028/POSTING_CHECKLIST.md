# WRR posting checklist

These items remain submission tasks. They are not scientific revision of packages A–D.

## Authors and affiliations
- [ ] Confirm author names, order, ORCIDs and corresponding author.
- [ ] Confirm affiliations and funding statements.
- [ ] Confirm conflicts of interest.
- [ ] Replace placeholder author blocks in the article and Supporting Information.

## Legal access for review
- [ ] Provide a permitted reviewer path to source observations used in the 42-station evaluation.
- [ ] Resolve LUBW reuse and redistribution before sharing estimator or calibration derivatives that include LUBW cases.
- [ ] Keep the 95-station open witness subset available for arithmetic audit of the 35 observation witnesses.
- [ ] Do not treat unresolved provider licences as open.

## Permanent deposit
- [ ] Assign a permanent repository and citation for code, processed tables and figure scripts.
- [ ] Deposit the frozen whole-archive predictions used for packages A–C.
- [ ] Deposit `results/wrr_process_review_20261028` summaries and the nested trajectory report.
- [ ] Record licence and access conditions beside each deposited archive.

## Closed scientific items
Passed plots, denominators and method-specification revisions stay closed. Do not reopen them to complete this checklist.
