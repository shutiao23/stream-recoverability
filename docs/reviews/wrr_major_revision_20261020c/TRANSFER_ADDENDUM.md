# Hydrologically disjoint source transfer

Declared before new calibration and test outcomes are opened. In addition to
the original source-only, provider-excluded, local-recalibration and local-fit
conditions, fit the four predefined principal comparators on USGS records only,
with calibration groups also limited to USGS. Freeze and apply them to the new
Swiss and French records. River catchments in the North American source and
European target cannot share river-flow connections. This supplies a stricter
source-target hydrological separation than station identity alone.

Other transfer conditions retain their stated interpretation. Station-disjoint
records in European source and target archives need not have hydrologically
disjoint upstream catchments, particularly when the old archive lacks catchment
polygons. Known candidate catchment overlap and shared input stations remain
grouped or excluded. Do not claim full hydrological independence for those
other conditions. Climate correlation and distribution shift also mean that the
independent-group risk theorem is not automatically a guarantee on this target.

This is a predefined additional robustness condition, not a replacement for a
failed primary H2 comparison. Model choice, tolerance, margin and the original
salted target roles do not change. No target error was used to choose this
source restriction.
