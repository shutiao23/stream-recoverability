# Controlled contrasts and admission are different experiments

Declared before new calibration/test outcomes are opened. The broadened 3x3
crossed panel is a mechanism experiment with 30-, 60- and 90-day gaps. Using it
as the primary admission population would omit short outages and can make a
length rule reject everything. Its classifier results are retained as
controlled-regime diagnostics, not as the primary archive-admission headline.

The primary admission benchmark instead regenerates the existing outage design
on all eligible opened records: one feasible climatological-overlap class for
each length 14, 30, 60, 90, 120, 180 and 270 days, plus up to five empirical
geometries. This remains a deliberately mixed assay, not the natural outage
prevalence. Empty empirical pools and infeasible geometries remain absent.
Prospective geometry pools use historical years only. All competing scores
receive this same population, reconstruction, training and calibration budget.
Natural-calendar replay and event/cofailure assays are separate tests. No
primary margin, comparator, loss, target role or tolerance changes.

The residual sampler also handles interpolation gaps shorter than its nominal
14-day block: only when no 14-day residual block exists, it may use a complete
block as long as the longest missing run. It never falls back to independent
singletons for a long missing run. This corrects a missing-uncertainty artifact
for genuine short interpolation gaps; it is not an assumption of zero error.

Primary final-protocol model fitting, transfer and power use the admission
cohort. The earlier controlled-regime score fits remain archived diagnostics.
Neither cohort is an independent confirmation because the opened source
records have already supported method development.

## Stronger common-information and whole-system references

Before new calibration/test outcomes, add an aligned certificate score for the
fixed information-priority reconstruction, historical mask-matched H30 error,
scaled climatological overlap times actual reconstruction residual scale, and
a same-capacity model containing complete observed and historical diagnostics.
These prevent a weak proxy or omitted decision-time statistic from creating a
spurious representation advantage. They supplement the fixed primary comparator,
not replace it after seeing results.

Also evaluate a whole-system certificate reference that chooses its own
reconstructor and calibrates the error of that chosen point. Its delivered
point is different, so it is explicitly separate from the common-point feature
ablation. The estimator choice uses decision-time features and candidate
predictions only; its hidden labels are used for fitting/calibration or final
evaluation, never to select a reconstructor at deployment. All original
candidate records, unavailable points and abstentions remain in its denominator.

The final no-screen composition test inherits the previously opened length and
support bins, but fits rates on opened-source empirical geometries only and
freezes them before new-test calendar replay. Bin discovery is not independent;
the new station histories are. Point advice is not risk certification. Source
bootstrap variability and the small number of independent target components
are reported separately. Compound low-flow/heat labels use training-month flow
quantiles and are distinguished from the published two-day event catalog.
