# Pre-test power and stopping decision

The opened-source admission experiment, not the controlled long-gap panel,
provides the planning distribution. Both reconstruction protocols completed
1,000 full training/calibration resamples of four paired learners. Local daily
fits are cached because resampling an entire independent component leaves its
historical inputs unchanged; the global learner and empirical threshold are
refitted. Prospective fitting/calibration use years through 2018 and evaluation
uses 2019--2022. The three fold seeds are not independent experiments.

The primary five-point gain is not consistently supported on opened sources;
direct H30 uncertainty explains essentially all of the uncertain-window gain.
The fully prospective source planning interval also includes no gain. Therefore
we do not expand the acquisition until a preferred design wins. Retain the
pre-value salted 53-station cohort, including seven documented-daily-mean Swiss
test components and twelve separately reported French test components. The
seven metadata-only USGS catalogs are not promoted into temperature acquisition
after observing these source results. No new target test error informed this
decision.

Power projections are conditional on source-like independent groups and
location-shift alternatives. They do not prove that both competing methods meet
the risk cap under transfer. Report all seven/19/27/53/100/200/400-group
projections and the 80%/90% planning thresholds; do not describe more masks as
more power. Proving a gain strictly above five points has only size-level power
when the true gain is exactly five points, regardless of sample size. Use the
predeclared eight- and ten-point alternatives to discuss such superiority.

The independent target result may therefore be inconclusive or negative. That
is an allowed outcome, not permission to change the comparator, practical
margin, endpoint, target split or selected-risk semantics. The paper will be
framed as a fit-for-use study if the representation advantage is not established.
