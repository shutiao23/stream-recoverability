Subject: Question about daily observation completeness, time basis and reuse for RivTemp data

Dear RivTemp team,

We are preparing a study on the recovery of sustained river-temperature maxima from incomplete records. We are exploring whether some RivTemp records could provide an independent reference, and would appreciate clarification before selecting stations or evaluating predictions.

1. For the RivTemp--MFFP_QC dataset available through DataStream (DOI 10.25976/sz2k-b624), can the number of valid underlying observations and the expected sampling interval be obtained for each daily mean? Can a day with a complete 24-hour observed record be distinguished from a day that meets the more-than-90% completeness threshold but has missing intervals?
2. What time zone and day-boundary convention apply to these daily observations, including daylight-saving transitions and older station records? Are there station-specific changes to the convention?
3. Which point-level quality or estimated-value flags, sensor change records and station watershed identifiers are available for determining whether at least four complete, nonestimated reference years exist at a station?
4. The DataStream page displays an ODC-BY licence as well as a disclaimer stating that the database cannot be transferred in whole or in part to another organization. Please clarify the intended conditions for redistribution of original and derived station-year values, reviewer-only access, publication in a trusted repository and required attribution.
5. What process should researchers follow to request access to station metadata and observations, and may the detailed selection and quality criteria be documented publicly without redistributing the restricted records?

We would preserve the distinction between daily summaries and underlying subdaily measurements, acknowledge RivTemp and the contributing organizations, and provide a reviewable description of any use consistent with your terms.

Thank you for your guidance.
