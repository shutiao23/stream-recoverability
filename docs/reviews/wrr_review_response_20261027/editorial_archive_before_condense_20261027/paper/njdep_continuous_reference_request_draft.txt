Subject: Clarification of long-term freshwater temperature data and time conventions

Dear monitoring team,

We are assessing whether quality-reviewed continuous river-temperature observations could support an independent study of sustained annual heat extremes. We would appreciate clarification before interpreting or redistributing any station data.

1. The download page contains some river/stream entries labeled as marine water, and a duplicate station identifier is associated with both freshwater and marine labels. Which metadata field should be used to identify genuinely freshwater river sites and distinguish duplicate station deployments?
2. A number of freshwater entries have no displayed QA start or end date. Does a blank range mean that no quality-reviewed observations exist, or only that the metadata range is unavailable? Is there a current inventory of freshwater temperature series and complete deployment intervals?
3. For downloaded files labeled EST, are time stamps fixed UTC-05 throughout the year, local civil time with daylight-saving changes, or another convention? Are timestamps beginning-of-interval, end-of-interval or instantaneous?
4. For the temperature data, how are missing values, estimated or interpolated observations, edits and sensor drift represented? Are original sampling cadence, calibration checks and per-record or per-deployment quality flags available?
5. May original and derived station-day data be shared with journal reviewers and deposited for publication? Please identify applicable licensing, redistribution and attribution conditions.

Our preliminary, availability-selected check of four stations and 2016–2019 files found missing reported date labels in each station-year. This is a limited screening outcome, not an assessment of the quality of the monitoring program or other sites and years.
