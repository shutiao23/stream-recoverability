\section{Trend sensitivity to annual interval expansion}
After examining the source-only annual correction and the earlier trend results, we propagated it through the original 256-draw normal-spacing quantile representation. The endpoint-only scheme moves the 5th and 95th quantiles while holding the five interior quantiles fixed. The tapered scheme also shifts the 10th, 25th, 75th and 90th quantiles by 0.8, 0.4, 0.4 and 0.8 times the radius, respectively; the median and complete-year values are unchanged. Both schemes use the same adjusted 90\% endpoints and respect observed lower bounds. Their interior and normal-spacing outer tails differ. The same independent, auxiliary shared-parameter and residual-block rank matrices are reused within each scheme; no calibrated joint distribution is implied. Among 820 forward controlled-gap station--repetition settings, 760 belong to regions with source support for both methods. Seventy-three of these lack at least one annual distribution, and two have no incomplete year, leaving 685 common nontrivial settings. Original saved trend intervals are reproduced before adjustment. The target selection and calibration remain retrospective.
