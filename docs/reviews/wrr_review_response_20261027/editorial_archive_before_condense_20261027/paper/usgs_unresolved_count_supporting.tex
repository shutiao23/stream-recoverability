\section{Additional USGS count-only candidate screen}
An additional metadata-only roster of 10 stations was fixed before requesting 2010--2024 approved daily-mean counts. All retained unresolved river-network identities; no temperatures or reconstruction errors were saved. No station had at least four full years in the initial screen. After that result, the same entire roster was checked for 2025; this extension is post hoc and unregistered. No station is confirmed to pass the four-year count requirement, and 1 remains undetermined because the 2025 statistic was not returned. Three other missing 2025 statistics cannot change eligibility because their original counts are at most two. For the undetermined candidate, the current metadata end precedes 2025, but metadata may lag observations and alternative series were not screened. Count completeness does not establish nonestimated daily observations, complete subdaily coverage, sensor accuracy, river-network independence or climate independence. No candidate was scored.
