\section{Source-provider dependence of whole-archive models}
The 42 whole-archive target stations belong to providers with recorded reuse paths, but source-model training has additional provenance. Recomputing the source role assignments and checking them against all 13 saved seed-zero model bundles shows that 13 estimator roles and 6 interval-calibration roles have positive-weight LUBW source cases. Source cases are repeated masks; their counts are not independent observations. Positive source weights establish model dependence but do not resolve the legal status of any particular derivative. Individual source, model, target, mask and reviewer-access rights still require confirmation. A post-hoc candidate-provider strict-region refit is reported separately and does not replace the main results.
