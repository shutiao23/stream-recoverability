Subject: Clarification of historical water-temperature quality codes and use

Kia ora Greater Wellington Environmental Monitoring team,

We are evaluating whether previously retrieved river water-temperature records
can support a scientific reference dataset. We would be grateful for guidance
about the Hilltop StdSeries and StdQualSeries records for the following sites:
Akatarawa River at Cemetery; Hutt River at Taita Gorge; Kopuaranga River at
Stuarts; Mangaone Stream at Ratanui; Mangaroa River at Te Marua; Waitohu Stream
at Water Supply Intake; Wainuiomata River at Leonard Wood Park; Wainuiomata
River at Manuka Track; and Waipoua River at Mikimiki Bridge.

The cached records span approximately 2001–2026, depending on site. Could you
please advise:

1. What do historical StdQualSeries codes 0 and 1 mean for these continuous
   water-temperature series, and during what periods did those meanings apply?
   Is there a published data dictionary or crosswalk to the NEMS schema?
2. How should code 200 be interpreted for these records? Does a quality event
   apply forward until the next event? How are missing or default quality values
   represented?
3. Which observations are verified or provisional, and how are estimated,
   interpolated, corrected, or sensor-fault periods indicated? Is there a way to
   obtain the corresponding flags and calibration or maintenance history?
4. Are these historical timestamps consistently fixed New Zealand Standard
   Time (UTC+12), including during daylight saving, and were sampling interval
   changes or site/sensor relocations recorded?
5. What attribution, licence and redistribution conditions apply to archived
   records, derived daily and annual summaries, and trained model outputs?

Any documentation or a contact for the appropriate data steward would be very
helpful. Thank you for your time.
