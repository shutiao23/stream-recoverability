\section{Late-start USGS count-only screen}
After earlier candidate screens, we fixed an exploratory metadata-only roster of 13 sites with series beginning in 2017--2021 and ending during or after 2025; 11 of the 24 initial candidates had known previous contact and were excluded. The 2017--2025 approved annual sample-count screen confirmed at least four complete calendar years for 0 sites. This count does not prove that other sites lack such years: unreturned annual statistics are unknown. The roster was not prospective and was availability-selected after earlier screens. Counts may include estimated values and do not establish complete subdaily coverage, river suitability, network independence, measurement quality, or permission to reuse data. No daily temperature values or reconstruction errors were used.
