# Experiment registry

Everything below was fixed before the confirmatory run and is not renegotiable
after seeing a result. Exploratory work that preceded this freeze is labelled
as such and does not appear in the manuscript as evidence.

## Population and units

| item | value |
|---|---|
| population | river waterbodies with at least one fully observed calendar year |
| excluded by class | seas, lakes, canals, estuaries, deltas — listed, not averaged in |
| station eligibility | at least four fully observed calendar years |
| unit of analysis | one station-year under one outage realization |
| clustering unit for all inference | river network |
| reference series | never imputed; a year with any missing day supplies no truth |

## Split

| item | value |
|---|---|
| split level | network, never station-year |
| stratification | provider × donor regime (none / sparse / rich) |
| seed | 20260923, recorded before any scoring |

| resulting panels | 110 development, 36 reserved networks |
| reserved opened | once, on 2026-09-23, with the frozen model and no retuning |

## Endpoints

| endpoint | window | natural scale (interannual SD) | primary tolerance |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| hottest 30-day mean (primary) | 30 d | 1.06 °C | 0.50 °C (0.47 SD) |
| hottest 7-day mean | 7 d | recomputed | 0.47 SD |
| annual mean | 365 d | 0.55 °C | 0.47 SD |
| coldest 30-day mean | 30 d | 0.80 °C | 0.47 SD |
| amplitude | 30 d peak − 30 d trough | 1.36 °C | 0.47 SD |

Tolerances are a fixed fraction of each endpoint's own scale so the endpoints
are comparable. The fraction is swept at 0.25, 0.47 and 1.0 and the axis
ordering must be invariant across the sweep to be claimed.

## Estimators

Fixed set, no tuning after the freeze: observed-days (current practice),
day-of-year median climatology, smoothed climatology, linear interpolation,
donor ridge refit on the visible subset, air ridge where forcing exists.
Fitting is leave-one-year-out within the station; a strictly-prior-years
variant is a declared sensitivity, not an alternative headline.

## Baselines the result must beat

| baseline | why it is the right competitor |
|---|---|
| fixed outage-length threshold | the incumbent in published guidance |
| record-completeness fraction | the incumbent in agency standards |
| warm-season coverage threshold | the strongest single alternative axis |
| random retention | the floor |
| oracle retention | the ceiling |
| length rule paired with an oracle reconstruction | guards against a handicapped incumbent |

## Primary success criterion

Frozen in `results/v17_fitness/step9_freeze/SUCCESS_CRITERION_v17.json` before
the reserved panel was opened. At the retention of the matched fixed-length
rule, the screen must reduce the consequential-error rate by at least five
percentage points with a network-clustered bootstrap interval excluding zero.
Precision was computed in advance: the reserved panel resolves 1.44 points, so
the declared effect is 3.5× the resolution.

## Truthfulness red lines

These invalidate specific results and must be repaired before those results are
used. They do not invalidate the research question.

1. A feature that reads a day the outage hides.
2. A calibration or fitting set sharing a network with its test set.
3. A reserved-panel number produced by a model that was retuned after opening.
4. A case table that cannot be regenerated byte for byte, including row order.
5. A number in the manuscript that disagrees with its artifact.
6. An incumbent given weaker information than the proposed method.

Items 4 and 5 were violated during development, detected, repaired, and the
affected numbers discarded rather than reported. Both are now enforced by tests.

## What is *not* a red line

A result that does not go the way the hypothesis predicted. The registry
records the effect, its uncertainty, its scope and which claim it changes. The
claim is then resized. Four claims were resized or withdrawn this way and are
reported as such.
