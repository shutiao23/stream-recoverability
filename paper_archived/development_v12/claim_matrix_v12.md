## Claims explicitly NOT made in v12

- Model-agnostic recoverability: all ranking claims are model-conditional
  ("the curve ranks the model that produced it").
- Cross-network transfer as a generic property: the design is within-network
  historical stress testing replicated across an outcome-disjoint network
  panel; "transfer" language is restricted to (a) the surface's pure transfer
  to new networks (t04) and (b) per-cell source-to-target matrix cells (t05).
- Full-panel superiority over the simple model (paired station-gap difference
  is negative, -0.095 [-0.158, -0.028]; network +0.109 CI includes zero).
- Any use of the 365-day extrapolation without abstention.
- A single generic stress curve supporting multiple missingness mechanisms,
  model families, or decisions.
- Conditional covariance as a variance/SD estimator or a general lower bound
  on realized error.
- External preregistration of anything before protocol v3 is registered:
  v2 remains internally hash-bound, same-commit; v3 is drafted, not yet
  registered, and no v3 outcomes exist.
- Station removal or automatic filling.
