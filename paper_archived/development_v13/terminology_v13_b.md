# Terminology v13 (frozen labels for the restructured paper)

Replaces `paper/development_v12/terminology_v12.md`. Use these labels in the
manuscript, SI, figures, and review response. Do not relabel a split, tier,
estimand, or evidence role after seeing outcomes. Terms unchanged from v12
are marked (v12); revised and new terms are marked (v13).

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `model-conditional historical stress test` (v12) | Artificial gaps cut wholly inside the fitting years of a record and recovered with a named recovery model; the resulting curve inherits that model's error structure and ranks only that model's future error. Never "model-agnostic recoverability." |
| `within-network historical stress testing replicated across an outcome-disjoint network panel` (v12) | The study design. Stress curves are built per network and evaluated on networks whose outcomes were never used to build, tune, or select anything. Replaces "cross-network transfer." |
| `outcome-disjoint panel` (v12) | A panel whose networks have no recovery outcomes in any earlier panel (development, first, or second). QC-only reuse is disclosed separately and capped. Panel composition: development 55 networks; first 42; second 57 (US 32, CZ 15, NO 10). |
| `direct support` / `directly supported horizons` (v12) | Units whose gap duration is in {7, 30, 90, 180} days AND a fitting-period curve cell exists (exact local, or the documented station/network-duration fallbacks where counted). The 874-unit and 841-unit numbers in the second panel. |
| `exact local support` (v12) | Station x duration x season curve cell exists; tier 1 of the support hierarchy. Second panel: 841/874 units, network Spearman 0.887. |
| `station-duration support` (v12) | Station x duration cell exists, season collapsed (tier 2). Second panel: 9 units (2 networks); report count only. |
| `network-duration support` (v12) | Network x duration cell exists, station collapsed (tier 3). Second panel: 0 units. |
| `network-mean fallback` (v12) | Prediction is the network-wide mean of all fitting-period losses (tier 4); constant within a network, within-network rank undefined by construction. Second panel: 596 units = 572 horizon-unsupported + 24 direct-horizon without a station curve (CORRECTS the v11 "572 fallback" count, which missed the 24). Fallback performance: network 0.562, pooled 0.339 — does not rank within networks. |
| `unavailable` (v12) | No fitting-period support at all (tier 5); zero scored units in the panels. |
| `same-unit paired comparison` (v12) | Both predictors evaluated on identical unit subsets; differences bootstrapped by paired network resampling. The t01 fix for the v11 unequal-subset comparison. |
| `paired DeltaRho` (v12) | Bootstrap mean of per-draw (empirical - comparator) Spearman; network-level and station-gap-level versions. |
| `within-network rank` (v12) | Spearman computed after removing network means (residualized pooled), or per-network Spearman with >= 3-4 units; undefined in the fallback tier. Second panel direct: residualized pooled 0.936, median within-network 0.965. |
| `network-difficulty control` (v12) | A predictor that separates only network means (network-mean-only benchmark, pooled rho 0.326); shows the 0.805 is not between-network difficulty. |
| `station x horizon historical mean` (v13) | The **primary strongest fitting-record baseline** (t03 ladder rung r6_station_gap): mean fitting-period MAE per station x gap-horizon cell, with the documented network-gap/network fallback. The empirical predictor is this object plus season stratification, with identical information access and unit granularity. On the second-panel direct 874 units: pooled 0.942, network 0.763; the two predictors correlate 0.992 (0.9917 on direct units). The fairness control for every C1 statement; never omitted from the primary comparison table. |
| `persistence of local recovery difficulty` (v13) | The v13 headline: the fitting-period stress curve ranks future loss at directly supported horizons at least as well as the strongest fitting-record baseline (pooled 0.945 vs 0.942; network 0.805 vs 0.763; paired network DeltaRho +0.042 with CI spanning zero). NOT "big gains from seasonal stratification" and NOT "uniquely superior in magnitude." |
| `interpolation-capable support-aware risk surface` (v13; replaces v12 `continuous support-aware risk surface`) | REML hierarchical model of log(1+MAE) on fitting-period placements (100,397 rows) with monotone duration spline, cyclic DOY, covariates, network/station random effects; provides predictions, 90% intervals, and support status at any duration. The name carries the boundary: it is **interpolation-capable within the fitted duration range** (14/60 d: pooled 0.774, slope 1.025), NOT a "full-horizon risk estimator" (365-d rank 0.270, coverage 46.8%). |
| `interpolation` (v12, retained) | Surface predictions at 14 and 60 days, inside the fitted duration range (448 units; calibrated, slope 1.025). |
| `extrapolation` / `extrapolation boundary` (v12, retained) | Surface predictions at 365 days, beyond the fitted range (124 units; rank 0.270, coverage 46.8%): THE boundary; unsupported unless real fitting-period support exists at that horizon. |
| `extrapolation abstention` (v13) | Withholding point-release of extrapolated (365-d) units on support grounds: 8.6% of units carrying 28.9% of total loss; raises released-unit surface network Spearman to 0.691, R2 to 0.663. Justified for point release, NOT for loss-capturing budgets (the abstained loss mass is the largest-loss tail). |
| `abstention` (v12, retained) | Withholding a prediction/release on support or ambiguity grounds; counted as zero captured loss / zero gain in decision endpoints. Never inflates a budget metric. |
| `coverage` (v13) | Fraction of units (or networks) released after an abstention rule: e.g., 8.5% coverage = 123 units / 8 of 42 networks released under the support + ambiguity rule (91.5% abstained). Positive decision claims require a prespecified coverage floor. |
| `coverage floor` (v13) | Prespecified minimum coverage for an operational decision claim: >= 50% of units AND >= 60% of networks released. v13 contains no positive decision claim: the 8.5% model-selection result is below the floor by construction. |
| `abstention cost` (v13) | The loss (or regret) foregone by abstained units: e.g., abstaining the 124 extrapolated units removes 28.9% of total loss from triage scope; the abstained units under support+ambiguity carry 91.4% of the panel's min-loss mass. Reported alongside every coverage figure. |
| `coverage-regret curve` (v13) | Plot of released-unit network-balanced regret (or CapturedLoss) vs coverage as the abstention threshold varies (t09 abstention_curve_part2: ambiguity delta x support rule). The v13 reporting convention for decision results. |
| `loss-targeting utility` (v13) | Utility of a policy measured by captured loss: CapturedLoss@B / NDCG@B over score-ranked units (the fixed-budget triage framing; t09 Part 1). |
| `end-to-end decision utility` (v13) | The broader decision framing: triage, model selection, and downstream metric protection together, each with prespecified coverage, abstention rules, and a named baseline. v13's C3 structure. |
| `incremental benefit` (v13) | B = D(default) - D(model) - lambda*C, where D is a decision loss (captured-loss deficit, selection regret, or metric distortion), default is the named baseline (no-fill, climatology fill, best fixed family), and C is an abstention/coverage cost scaled by lambda. The v13 requirement for any decision claim: the baseline and the cost must be specified; a benefit without a default is not reported. |
| `CapturedLoss@B` (v12) | Fraction of total observed loss in the top-B% of units ranked by a policy; oracle = 1 for the true top set at B. |
| `NDCG@B` (v12) | Position-discounted normalized gain over the score-ranked list truncated at B. |
| `network-balanced regret` (v12) | Mean over networks of within-network mean (selected loss - best-family loss). Part-2 values: no-abstention 0.084 vs best fixed 0.081; support+ambiguity abstention 0.0067 [0.0019, 0.0120] at 8.5% coverage. |
| `deployable nested-CV selector` (v13) | A model-selection rule whose family choice and coverage are both chosen by nested cross-validation on fitting-period data (no evaluation outcomes), with the abstention rule and coverage floor fixed before deployment. The per-network average-CV comparator (0.038) is in-sample and NOT a deployable selector; the 8.5%-coverage result is a proof of concept on non-random networks, not a deployable selector. |
| `released units` (v12) | Units remaining after an abstention rule; comparators re-evaluated on the same released units for fair coverage-risk views. |
| `fit-period fit` / `simple descriptors` (v12) | Route-A linear model (gap length, target autocorrelation, donor R2, additive d/4 heuristic, nearest-donor correlation) with coefficients fit on fitting-period data only (development + first panel for the second-panel comparisons). |
| `model-source x model-target matrix` (v12) | Cells = Spearman between a fitting-period stress curve of source family and outer-evaluation losses of target family, on the same networks. |
| `engineered-feature block` (v12) | Linear/PCHIP boundary, seasonal-boundary ridge, donor ridge, XGBoost: shared difficulty (self 0.93-0.98, cross 0.72-0.98). |
| `pipeline-specific difficulty` (v12) | Outside the block: BiLSTM and air2stream families do not share the block's difficulty axis. |
| `cross-instance transfer` (v13; replaces `self-transfer` for the neural family) | Transfer from one trained instance of a family to outer losses of a DIFFERENT training instance (e.g., BiLSTM validation-window stress vs frozen BiLSTM predictions). The BiLSTM "self" cell is cross-instance, not same-instance; use `cross-instance BiLSTM-family transfer`: self 0.29-0.69, cross to block -0.24..0.28, neural vs XGBoost stress Spearman 0.067. Do not write "BiLSTM self-transfer." |
| `mechanism-matched curve` (v12) | Trial gaps generated with the same missingness mechanism as the evaluation gaps (multi-block, donor-synchronous, forcing, online, uniform, summer- or high-temperature-biased). |
| `support-destroying mechanism` (v12) | Missingness that removes recovery information (donors, future boundary, forcing): donor-synchronous, target+primary-covariate, online. A uniform curve applied to these collapses rank (0.88-0.98 -> 0.20-0.40). |
| `missingness harmonization` (v13) | Re-running the missingness matrix on ONE unified 12-network panel with one scoring convention. Required before matched-strength numbers are evidence: independent implementations diverge on the matched donor-synchronous cell (0.979 vs 0.490) though the mismatch-collapse direction is consistent across both (e.g., 0.979 -> 0.294; donor-sync curve on uniform gaps DeltaRho -0.846). |
| `expected Gaussian MAE` (v12) | sqrt(2/pi) x mean per-day conditional SD; the code-defined estimand of the operator column previously mislabeled "conditional-variance lower bound." Neither a variance nor an SD (t10). |
| `remainder (MAE excess)` (v12) | Realized MAE minus expected Gaussian MAE (0.165 -> 4.268 C, 7 -> 365 d). NOT identifiable as model error + drift; contains covariance misspecification, parameter-estimation error, non-Gaussianity, aggregation, finite-sample error. |
| `calibration slope (equal-network weighted)` (v12) | Weighted OLS of observed on predicted with weights 1/(rows in network); the paper's calibration convention. |
| `rolling-origin cutoff` (v12) | Outer chronological split at 60/70/80% of record years; stress curve built strictly inside each training block. |
| `history-length learning curve` (v12) | Network Spearman vs fitting history (2/4/6/8/full years): 0.608 / 0.872 / 0.916 / 0.938 / 0.944; minimum usable history ~4 years. |
| `deployment-matched curve` (v12) | Stress curve built with the full 70% training block (equal to deployment length); diagnostic only (requires the evaluation window), quantifies the 49%-vs-70% training-length gap (paired MAE diff 0.013 C; Spearman 0.989). |
| `thermal-metric distortion` (v12) | |truth record - record with gap days filled (reconstruction) or dropped (no-fill)| for ten thermal-regime metrics; reconstruction restores computability (no-fill leaves amplitude undefined in 20.9% of placements). Distortion is baseline-dependent: vs no-fill, reconstruction errors are 12-14% (annual mean, degree days, trend) to 30-37% (p90, amplitude, threshold days) of the no-fill baseline; vs a climatology-fill baseline, risk-targeted budgets worsen threshold/extreme metrics (exceed_25_days -0.43) on long summer gaps (cold peak bias). |
| `evidence roles` (v13) | The four provenance classes attached to every result: (1) `frozen pre-outcome` = first-panel analyses and the second panel (internally hash-bound, frozen before outcome scoring; NOT external preregistration); (2) `post-hoc v12 development` = all revision analyses on already-scored panels (paired comparisons, support hierarchy, surface, matrices, rolling origin, downstream, decision experiments); (3) `v13 harmonization` = re-runs on unified panels/conventions required before evidence (missingness matrix); (4) `preregistered` = future third-panel protocol v3/v4 outcomes (none exist). Never attribute preregistered status to (1) or (2). |
| `protocol v3` (v12, retained) | Third-confirmation protocol: 80-120 outcome-disjoint networks; external timestamping (separate pre-outcome commit + OSF/Zenodo registration before outcomes); frozen margins; primary endpoints paired network-level DeltaRho on direct-support units (+0.038 observed; 80% power at N = 120), DeltaCapturedLoss@20%, NDCG@5%, thermal-metric protection floor (>= -0.02); full-panel within-network superiority vs simple NOT claimed (DeltaRho -0.093). Drafted, not yet registered; no v3 outcomes exist. |
| `protocol v4` (v13) | The decision-endpoint extension of protocol v3 required for ANY positive decision claim in v13: adds the coverage floors (>= 50% units / >= 60% networks), the abstention rules and abstention costs, the coverage-regret curves, the incremental-benefit endpoints B = D(default) - D(model) - lambda*C with named baselines, and the deployable nested-CV selector definition, all frozen before third-panel outcomes. Not drafted; no v4 outcomes exist. |
| `v2 confirmation` (v12) | Internally hash-bound 57-network second panel; amendment and outcomes share one version-control commit; NOT externally verifiable preregistration. Provenance description only; never appears in the abstract. |
| `station retention / automatic filling` (v12) | Unsupported actions; no certified safe-fill set or placement margin exists (v2 triage released zero; placement regret 6.0% directional without margin). |
