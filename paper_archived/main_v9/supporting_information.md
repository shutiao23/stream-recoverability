# Supporting Information

## Pre-fit information risk for stream-temperature gap recoverability across unseen river networks

**Lineage:** `main_v9`
**Charter:** `configs/design_freeze_v9.yaml`
**Executable protocol:** `configs/design_freeze_v10_executable.yaml`
**Evidence status:** confirmatory scoring not performed

This Supporting Information records the complete executable design, development
diagnostics, and evaluate-once QC failure. It contains no v4 case-study result
and does not convert development analyses into confirmation.

## Text S1. Independent units and split

The independent extrapolation unit is a whole river network. Stations are
nested within networks; site-years are nested within stations; masks and
placements are repeated measurements. The canonical split is frozen in
`configs/network_catalog_v3_split.yaml`. Jinsha and Chattahoochee are burned
historical/development cases and cannot enter the sealed confirmation.

The post-QC inventory contains 99 unique qualified networks: 47 development,
20 validation, and 32 sealed. The frozen network-interval floor is 100 and the
sealed absolute floor is 40. Neither floor was met. The complete qualified
catalog, 78-row exclusion ledger, and balance report are generated by
`scripts/104_build_qualified_network_catalog.py`.

## Text S2. Operator definition and synthetic identification

The operator uses fitting-period conditional covariance. The historical
additive $d/4$ heuristic remains only a weak comparator. Known failure modes
include shared-information double counting, Jensen error from evaluating ACF at
an average boundary distance, donor-count inflation, covariance instability,
and state-shift mismatch.

E0 synthetic rivers recover the expected memory- and donor-dominant signs. The
locked Twin E holdout gives operator Spearman 0.936 but calibration slope 0.760,
outside the frozen 0.9--1.1 synthetic band. The generator was not retuned. This
is a negative identification result, not real-river confirmation.

## Text S3. Same-model information coalitions

The primary information coalitions are boundary (B), synchronous donor (D),
meteorology (M), and hydraulics (H). W7 evaluates B, D, and B∪D using the same
frozen model rule, mask, site-year, and evaluation unit. M and H remain unbound
because timestamp-safe auxiliary inputs were not materialized for the frozen
corpus. Missing M/H cells are structural missingness in the experiment and are
not treated as zero information or imputed from temperature outcomes.

The W8 failure rule was triggered: operator incremental $R^2$ over donor-R²-only
was $6.88\times10^{-5}$ on the development slice. Operator, covariance, and
synthetic-generator retuning are forbidden.

## Text S4. Empirical and adversarial outage geometry

The frozen open-role geometry contains 2,355 empirical 7--180-day outages with
nonoverlapping, truth-bearing observed counterparts and 5,406 adversarial
stress cells. Adversarial cells include record-edge gaps, donor-thin windows,
and synchronous whole-network outages. Actual naturally missing days have no
truth and are never scored.

The development T4 comparison uses network as the outer unit and withholds its
interval below 100 networks. Natural and artificial geometries have different
internal weighting and are not declared equivalent.

## Text S5. Power, attrition, and Tier-2 sensitivity

The development power simulation reaches power 0.9125 at its largest recorded
20-network setting, but empirical corpus attrition still leaves the study below
the network floor. The fixed Tier-2 sample also attrited below its allowed
range. SAITS and CSDI were run on 14 qualified development networks across
30/90/180-day gaps. Air2stream and GRIN obligations were not executed, so a
complete structural-SOTA sensitivity is not claimed and the sample is not
reselected.

## Text S6. Evaluate-once QC ceremony

The once-lock was created before sealed reads. The production reader consumed
all 2,880 registered objects. QC retained 32 networks: 29 USGS HUC8 and 3 FOEN.
Because 32 is below the frozen sealed floor of 40, fixed-model recovery scoring
was not run. The production authorization is consumed. There is no sealed
Spearman, calibration slope, bias, placement utility, triage utility, coverage,
or interval score.

Rerunning the lock, replacing attrited networks, lowering either floor, or
scoring the retained 32 as confirmation is prohibited.

## Text S7. Topology, regulation, and causal boundary

The frozen matched analysis yields only two unique regulated-control network
pairs and does not support formal confound control. No new caliper or rematching
was introduced after outcomes. Regulation, groundwater, climate, topology, and
network size remain descriptive modifiers. Reservoir causation is forbidden.

## Text S8. Online-causal estimand

The online workload is separate from offline historical reconstruction. It
fits target information strictly before gap start, forbids the right boundary
and post-gap target, and permits only donor or forcing values available on or
before each prediction day. The workload contains 1,384,025 registered items;
a bounded ten-item smoke run verified execution and structural-not-applicable
model identities. Full execution is NO-GO below the network floor and without
timestamp-safe M/H.

## Text S9. Claim license

The following claims are not licensed:

- the operator predicts recoverability on unseen river networks;
- the operator adds value beyond donor R²;
- operator-based placement or triage improves monitoring decisions;
- static covariance provides calibrated uncertainty;
- reservoir operation caused a memory-dominated recoverability regime.

The current v9 lineage is a protocol and attrition/falsification record, not a
submittable confirmatory paper.

## Text S10. Reservoir-operations availability

The modern USGS time-series metadata API is reachable and exposes public
reservoir-storage series (parameter 00054). Storage availability does not
provide release temperature, outlet depth, aligned operations, heat flux, or a
matched control river. The availability audit therefore continues to set
`can_write_reservoir_cause=false`.
