# Main figures and generated artifacts

The workflow, calibration, mechanism, placement, and adaptation artifacts are
generated by `scripts/124_run_reviewer_completion.py`; Figure 5 is generated by
`scripts/139_run_us_heterogeneity.py`. All are under
`results/development_v11/reviewer_completion/`. Colors distinguish domain,
never dozens of individual networks. Captions lead with the scientific result.

## Figure 1 — Fitting-period evidence to decision

`figure_01_workflow.png` shows the four-stage argument: fitting-period
descriptors and artificial gaps, cross-network loss calibration, new-network
rank and interval evaluation, then placement or triage. Conditional covariance
is labelled as a lower bound rather than the novel endpoint.

## Figure 2 — First-confirmation calibration

`figure_02_confirmation_calibration.png` shows the complete-panel
simple-descriptor predictor with log-log axes, two color-blind-safe domain
colors, outlined network medians, a 1:1 line, and a residual panel. The caption
conclusion is: ordering transfers within networks, while magnitude calibration
shifts outside the US domain.

## Figure 3 — Saturation mechanism

`figure_03_mechanism.png` fixes the 61-station composition across all seven
horizons and plots conditional-variance lower bound, simple prediction, and
realized loss. The analytic curve saturates while observed long-gap loss grows.

## Figure S1 — Development station-retention replay

`figure_04_placement_replay.png` reports realized worst-target MAE regret above
the oracle across every feasible retained fraction for simple-risk minimax,
mutual information, QR pivoting, even spacing, and random placement. Only
networks with at least five stations and a complete directed replay matrix
enter; the small achieved network count is stated in the caption.

This artifact is supporting-only because the 14-network comparison was
specified after the first-panel analysis. The 13-network second replay is
reported numerically rather than substituted into this development figure; it
had no prespecified utility margin.

## Figure 4 — Domain reliability and adaptation cost

`figure_05_domain_adaptation.png` is labelled Figure 4 in the submission and
shows common-axis reliability curves for US
and cross-domain networks, then the fraction of network-grouped resamples whose
post-adaptation calibration slope lies in [0.9, 1.1] by requested labelled-row
budget. This is explicitly post-confirmation method development.

## Figure 5 — US climate and regulation heterogeneity

`figure_06_us_heterogeneity.png` shows adjusted calibration slopes and 95%
intervals from network random-intercept-and-slope models for simple and
empirical risk. Climate groups use broad HUC2 strata; regulation uses GAGES-II
major-dam presence where known. The display is descriptive and retains phase
interactions and mixed-model diagnostic warnings.
