A further experiment on previously opened data separates the conditional H30 law from its delivered numerical point. It retains the same controlled crossed-contiguous Swiss cases as the earlier reconstruction factorial; these masks do not estimate natural outage prevalence. Seven laws retain one priority point, including raw blocks, visible shifts, seasonal slow-plus-short residuals with and without available air, a separate flow tier, and the preserved air32 neural law with and without the same visible shift. Residual laws supply 512 nested draws for sampling diagnostics; all primary law, score and use comparisons use 32 draws. Observations remain fixed and residual histories exclude each target year. This is retrospective supplementation, not a change to the frozen prospective predictor.

CRPS on the common development cases selects the residual model conditioned on season and air temperature. On 907 common old Swiss test cases, its CRPS is 0.210 versus 0.273 degrees Celsius for raw blocks, and nominal-90\% coverage is 85.7\% versus 71.3\% (Tables~\ref{tab:state_risk_law_quality}--\ref{tab:state_risk_sampling}). At the unchanged priority point, its native CDF rule accepts 450 cases with 8 failures, while the corresponding window head accepts 494 with 19 failures. These empirical operating points neither establish the joint five-point advantage against both direct references nor certify risk.

The aggregate improvement does not resolve warm-year errors. Across 357 common warm-year cases at 32 draws, the selected residual law's H30 median bias is -0.379 degrees Celsius and coverage is 77.6\%; the neural reference has bias -0.330 and coverage 78.2\%. With 512 draws the selected residual law reaches 90.0\% aggregate coverage, showing an effect of the finite empirical-band budget. Its warm-year coverage remains 84.6\% and bias -0.379 degrees Celsius. These overlapping diagnostic conditions do not identify a physical cause or demonstrate transfer to an unseen climate or event.

The system experiment also changes numerical point while refitting each point's own error and calibrated admission. Four points, two same-air laws and three score types give 24 cells; a separate flow tier adds 18. The 945-candidate denominator is retained for every decision, while common-subset MAE has 828 same-air or 821 flow-supported cases. Improved point error does not by itself validate a CDF tolerance event or admission threshold. All 63 formal rules across the law and system experiments have empty admission. Conditional warm/flow checks and full expected-cost references remain supplementary, with unchanged original H1--H3 and no new independent confirmation.
