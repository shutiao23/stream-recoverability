Calibration-set composition also matters. With the developed model and heads held fixed, all 247 subsets of three, five, seven or nine target calibration groups yield 741 score/subset evaluations on the same 945 tests. With three groups, the native CDF rule has empty admission in 28 of 84 subsets; its nonempty test risks range from 1.0\% to 24.4\%. Even seven-group subsets reach a sample risk of 6.2\%. Every formal calibration remains empty. These ranges measure conditional sensitivity to already opened calibration groups, not a powered independent test; no subset is selected to improve the result.
