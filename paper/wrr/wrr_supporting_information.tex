\section{Models, information and uncertainty}
Matched quantile boosting uses 350 iterations, depth four, learning rate 0.06, minimum leaf size forty and quantile 0.9. Generic features contain gap geometry, season, visible donors, training climatological variability, historical record length, boundary observations, air availability and H30-estimator disagreement. Actual reconstruction OOF residual diagnostics, mask-matched historical H30 errors and direct H30 uncertainty are stronger decision-time alternatives, not hidden evaluation truth. Full window features add probabilistic support, its spread, peak-start spread/entropy, competition and interactions. Exact allowlists are in the analysis contracts; truth, true overlap and realized errors are forbidden predictors.

Spline models add a five-knot cubic basis to the same small information set. The two-point equivalence margin is distinct from five-point superiority. Conditional source intervals lying inside the former do not demonstrate whole-procedure or cross-population equivalence.

Residual trajectories preserve observed target values. Training residuals are generated by holding out each permitted historical year as well as the current target year. Fourteen-day blocks cannot cross NaN boundaries. Short interpolation gaps may use complete blocks equal to their longest run only when no nominal block exists. Unsupported uncertainty is explicit missing information; it is not zero uncertainty. Ninety-percent scenario bands are not promised coverage and are evaluated as such.

Certificate references retain their declared minimum training support. Some locally fitted certificate heads are unavailable with the new development budget. Those are not successful zero-risk predictions and cannot establish an architectural win for another method. A selected certificate's delivered point and error differ from a certificate-derived ranking applied to the fixed priority point.

\section{Risk estimand and proof}
For a fixed predictor and candidate threshold, let each deployment component contribute within-component mean admission $A_g$ and failure-and-admission $B_g$. Since $0\leq B_g\leq A_g\leq1$,
\[
 X_g=B_g+r(1-A_g)\in[0,1],\qquad r=0.05.
\]
The desired ratio is $E[B_g]/E[A_g]$, not mean component-conditional risk and not arbitrary case-weighted risk. For $G$ independent deployment-representative components, invert the bounded-variable Chernoff--KL inequality to obtain an upper mean bound $U$ satisfying
\[
 G\,\mathrm{kl}(\bar X,U)=\log(m/\alpha),
\]
where $m$ is the number of training-fixed candidate thresholds. A union bound covers candidate search, including nonmonotone risk. If any threshold has $U\leq r$ and positive admission, then $E[B_g]\leq r E[A_g]$ on the simultaneous-validity event. No result follows under arbitrary target shift or dependent components. The zero-mean upper bound is $1-(\alpha/m)^{1/G}$, strictly positive.

Even the ideal all-admit, zero-failure observation therefore needs \RSingleThresholdGroups{} groups for one candidate or \RGridThresholdGroups{} for twenty-one. Target calibration is too small. Empty certified admission is insufficient evidence, not observed risk zero or a recommendation to discard every record.

The empirical bootstrap rule is a separate descriptive operating policy. Constructed populations include twelve, forty and one hundred perfectly dependent-within-group cases and a rare high-risk group, with uninformative scores. These are counterexamples to general calibration claims, not measured frequencies in rivers. Test intervals describe variation over components conditional on fixed predictors; they cannot supply probability for an unobserved failure type.

\section{Freezing and time}
Comparative source folds are 60/20/20 fitting/calibration/testing. Final source heads use an independent source calibration split, fitting on the other source components. F0 transfers the head and source threshold; F1 changes only target calibration; F2 trains on new development records and calibrates on the target calibration role. \section{Reconstruction references}
The CSDI reference has two layers, two heads, 32 channels, fifty diffusion steps, a 366-step single feature, mixed random/structured masks, maximum thirty epochs and patience five. It is trained on documented old-source records only, with independent old validation groups and one metadata-chosen validation station-year per group. Target normalization excludes the evaluated year. Eight conditional draws provide a pointwise median series and a secondary median of trajectory H30 values. It is a fixed retrospective reference, not optimized state of the art or a prospective model.

Air2stream-3/8 use the existing published-equation implementation with compiled numerical kernels verified against it. Parameters fit the longest permitted contiguous forcing segment excluding the target year, and strictly before it prospectively. Discharge normalization uses training forcing only. Each interior gap starts from the last observed left anchor, never hidden truth. Incomplete forcing, unavailable discharge, insufficient training and nonfinite predictions are explicit statuses. Optimizer status and training error are retained. Comparisons show input requirements, availability and fixed-policy error on exactly the same supported cases.

\section{Decisions, unknowns and representation}
All operating policies begin with an identical candidate pool within an assay. Unknown or unsupported answers remain in its denominator. Local hot thresholds use permitted training-year H30 quantiles; fixed 18 and 20-degree sensitivity thresholds are not regulatory claims. Full false-positive/negative, correct/unknown hot/cool counts, near-threshold outcomes and network coverage are in original-pool CSVs.

The cost grid charges every unknown record and assumes no perfect repair. Intervals are conditional descriptive component-bootstrap intervals, not simultaneous inference across all costs or measured ecological values. Selected hot-frequency error is distinct from original-pool unknown-label bounds. Those bounds allow all unknown labels to be cool or hot and do not correct mistakes in reported labels.

A post-test deterministic baseline audit adds always-unknown, always-hot and always-cool policies to the unchanged cost grid. Always unknown has loss exactly the abstention cost; it produces no magnitude prediction and no finite selected-risk estimate. This does not alter the original H3 comparator, but prevents an improvement over a weak reconstruct-all reference being presented as operational superiority. Point dominance in selected sensitivity cells is not a new confirmatory endpoint.

The largest fully observed window mean is a deterministic lower bound on the complete recorded maximum. It can retain a hot label without accepting H30 magnitude, but cannot establish a cool label. An eighty-percent mean is not a bound. Fixed observation margins are sensitivity scenarios, not provider error specifications. The extension changes no magnitude error event.

\section{Power, stopping and a nonpredictive correction}
Each protocol has 1,000 complete learner/calibration refits. Entire overlap components are resampled, preserving all station histories and masks. Deterministic local fits are cached because their inputs do not change under whole-component resampling; global heads and thresholds are refitted. Evaluation components are also resampled for whole-procedure intervals. Learning curves use twenty subsets per non-full size; full-pool seed repeats have one distinct subset.

Power projections compare predeclared five-, eight- and ten-point alternatives with zero and five-point null margins. They assume source-like group distributions and do not establish common-risk feasibility. An effect exactly at a superiority boundary has only size-level rejection, not high power. Training/calibration uncertainty limits projections even with more test groups. No additional stations were acquired until a preferred method won.

An initial descriptive label mistakenly treated snow percentages as fractions. Correcting majority snow to fifty percent, with glacier influence first and unknown attributes explicit, affects no feature, fitted model, calendar, eligibility, component or frozen role. Original locks and outputs remain unchanged. Script 373 supplies corrected descriptive subgroup tables; raw script-360 hydrological labels are superseded. A descriptive near-natural string in an earlier case manifest is not verified regulation information and supports no unregulated-catchment claim. The dated erratum records when this correction was made.
