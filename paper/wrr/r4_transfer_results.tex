The 669 retrospective Swiss masks represent 105 distinct station-years in seven stations, with 17 distinct hot station-years; they are generated single gaps retaining annual boundary observations. Sixteen of 33 frozen admitted failures occur at CH\_2327, Dischmabach--Davos, Kriegsmatte, and all sixteen underestimate H30. Fifteen use air reconstruction. The official station inventory confirms a roughly 43-km$^2$ alpine catchment with 0.7\% glacial area \cite{foen2327}; this is not evidence of a specific regulation or groundwater class. Gap-period cold proxy errors can exceed visible-period errors, while low-flow overlap varies. Source donor removal raises unscreened error but does not reproduce the full Swiss distribution. Persistent peak-season proxy mismatch is a testable explanation, not an identified low-flow or melt mechanism \cite{michel2020}. All original groups remain in the primary result.
