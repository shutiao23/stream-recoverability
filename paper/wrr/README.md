## Reproduction

    make wrr-followup

Regenerate only current document evidence and PDFs with `make wrr-followup-evidence`, or check their numerical invariants and original locks with `make wrr-followup-check`. The follow-up uses previously opened outcomes, not a new blind test. It adds two sets of 1,000 full four-cell refits, explicit decision references and a reconstruction factorial whose unsupported cases remain unknown in original-pool utility.

The original scientific round can still be reproduced separately with:

    make wrr-round-20261020c

Expanded probability development, joint planning and public-backup qualification use `make -j2 wrr-probability-budget`, `make wrr-joint-power`, `make wrr-backup-resource`, then `make wrr-extended-evidence`. The two neural configurations share old fitting and validation data; their complete H30 law is supplied to all three numerical point arms. The source-like planning grid reruns learning, calibration and evaluation, but provides no supported 80% sample-size recommendation. Its larger simulated budgets are not acquired independent histories.

`make wrr-three-method-power` separately executes the developed-point three-method/two-reference procedure, with 3,000 complete score-head refits and 432 budget cells (SI Table S44). All cells have zero joint hits in 1,000 old-role empirical repetitions. This is planning under 11/9/7 opened role support groups, with the probability law frozen; it does not certify power or recommend recruitment for a new population.

## Interpretation and limits

- Mechanism controls and admission populations are separate; the latter includes short and empirical outages.
- Source labels are grouped through all target and donor identities; this does not prove independent climates.
- New continuous references have 21 development, 13 calibration and 19 test stations. Swiss documented means and French project aggregates are separate.
- F0 freezes source model and threshold; F1 changes only target calibration; F2 fits on new development stations and calibrates locally.
- The fully prospective head/calibration use years through 2018 and all upstream local inputs are earlier-year-only.
- This is a calendar-time forward hindcast with current-vintage observations/reanalysis, not an archived real-time-vintage field trial.
- Snow share is a percent. Corrected descriptive subgroup outputs do not change frozen predictions, roles or thresholds.
- Unsupported heads, missing forcing, unknown labels and empty admissions are not counted as successful zero-risk predictions.
- Original physical references are not substituted into frozen admission training. The added development factorial learns each reconstruction's own error and use decisions; its input tiers and limited probability reference do not establish universal system superiority.
- A public redundant-logger search supplies no natural case meeting the original historical-support protocol. Two short-history winter records with partial daily sampling are an explicitly separate sensitivity; their observed hot windows make them weak evidence for difficult outage recovery.
- The user has no additional backup or costly auxiliary material. Freely available archive and forcing data receive no invented query cost; the acquisition-policy branch remains inactive.
- Additional Australian reference qualification has not become a new evaluation: mapped qualified records occupy 33 conservative hydrologic blocks, six span original roles and one station remains pending topology review. Original polygon-component counts do not establish independent deployment units. See `make wrr-au-dependence-audit` and the generated SI caveat.
- Always-unknown/hot/cool costs are reported in a post-test deterministic baseline audit; beating the fixed reconstruct-all reference does not imply operational optimality.

Previous manuscript/response sources and PDFs are retained under [the 20261020b archive](../../paper_archived/wrr_20261020b/). They are not the current evidence.
