\section{Additional exploratory experiments}

\subsection{Outcome-blind year-set selection}

All feasible same-count subsets are enumerated while retaining every fixed complete year. There are \PETimeSubsets{} subsets across \PETimeOriginalCommon{} common archives; the \PETimeN{} actual-mask primary archives contribute \PETimePrimarySubsets{} subsets. The two zero-mask settings contribute one subset each. Only \PETimeChoiceN{} actual-mask archives offer a nontrivial choice; the remaining \PETimeIdenticalN{} have identical same-count selections by construction. CDF minimizes the retained-versus-full empirical-year-CDF squared distance, including complete years; Sxx maximizes the centered decade-year sum of squares of the entire retained set. Ties use the alternate geometry score and then the earliest incomplete-year tuple. No temperature or error enters either selector. They change 367 and 376 selected sets relative to warm-season screening, respectively. These post-review exploratory rules are not deployment recommendations and do not separately identify effects of year count and temporal position.

Uniform exact means $\sqrt{\operatorname{mean}_{a}E_{U\mid a}(E_a^2)}$, with an equal-probability set $U$ inside each archive $a$. It is not the mean RMSE over random selections. Over 200 random seeds, the mean RMSE is 0.4505, the root of mean sampled MSE is 0.4506, and the exact conditional root-expected-MSE is 0.4492 $^{\circ}$C per decade. The across-seed 2.5th--97.5th percentiles [0.4359, 0.4671] describe selection variation, not a confidence interval; 62/200 seeds improve on the warm-season point result. Paired intervals in Table~\ref{tab:time-support} instead resample the recorded connected groups 10,000 times, preserving paired archives and their conditional subset-average MSEs before taking roots.

For each fixed set $E=S+R$, hence $\operatorname{MSE}(E)=\operatorname{MSE}(S)+\operatorname{MSE}(R)+2\operatorname{mean}(SR)$. Averaging over uniform subsets preserves this identity; negative cross terms can cancel squared errors. Neither RMSE subtraction nor normalized squared terms are independent causal contributions.

\subsection{Larger-budget source-role calibration}

The source calibration union contains \PECalSourceCases{} cases at \PECalSourceStations{} stations and \PECalSourceYears{} unique station-years. The Swiss/Bavarian target roles contain \PECalSwissSourceGroups{}/\PECalBavariaSourceGroups{} positive-weight interval-calibration groups under the original saved role assignment. All source calibration years precede 2010; held target groups remain excluded and original integer bootstrap multiplicities are unchanged. For each seed and budget the order is seven raw source quantiles $\to$ observed-bound projection $\to$ weighted source residual order statistics; target raw quantiles $\to$ bound $\to$ add source offset $\to$ sort $\to$ bound. A cell requires at least 50 finite positive-weight cases before multiplicity; otherwise the finite positive calibration pool is used. No target truth is used for offset estimation.

Native20 calls the original bridge with 20 draws, whereas nested20 is the prefix of a 500-draw call; they need not be identical because the random arrays are generated by day. Native20 source and target references are checked separately against original case/repair and archive files. Seeds 0, 1 and 2 share fixed daily models, residual histories and masks. The uncalibrated, fixed-old-offset and updated-source-offset layers score the same target raw draws. D2 retains its original seven annual quantiles, with no refit or recalibration. Every CRPS uses the common bounded normal-spacing 256-point annual approximation; these are annual scores, not newly computed full-archive trends. The 27 target years span only two connected groups; 500 paths do not prove convergence or broad ranking robustness.

\subsection{Regional and annual water--air influence diagnostics}

Removing a connected group and recomputing common cells changes both support and the standardized target population; it is an influence check, not a fixed-population causal contrast. Geometry min-count coverage gaps stay positive, whereas the smaller geometry-plus-reconstructor subset can reverse sign. Training-history imbalance and the missing Swiss air-ridge counterpart remain. Annual water--air sensitivity also depends on whether years or stations receive equal total weight. A group deletion can leave only one Swiss station, and the Bavarian aggregate change masks group influence. None of these checks establishes daily reconstruction stability or equivalence.

\subsection{Same-day missingness with an evaluable-peer denominator}

Peer-evaluable rows require another station in the same connected group with an eligible recorded missingness pattern in that year. Observed peers use the eligible-year roster, which includes incomplete years; trial peers use the complete-reference-year roster on which masks are imposed. Group-years with no such peer are excluded and counted. Same-day overlap means at least one missing day coincides with a peer missing day. Shared station-days count each focal station's missing days that coincide with any peer, divided by its eligible missing station-days. Observed rows are incomplete station-years; trial rows are station-year--replicates. Same-day co-missingness does not identify an outage cause, the donor actually used, or a naturally unobserved failure process.
