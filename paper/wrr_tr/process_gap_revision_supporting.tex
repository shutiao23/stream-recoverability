\subsection{Common gap metric and archive count audit}

The primary comparison uses 2010--2025 to match the forward experiment. The observed eligible archive covers 41 forward-cohort stations; only 29 have incomplete years in this period. There are 109 unique incomplete station-years, without artificial mask repetition. The empirical-calendar and controlled-gap summaries contain 3,806 and 3,777 incomplete station-year--replicate rows, respectively, across the same 41-station forward cohort. Their repeated appearances preserve experimental weighting and do not create new independent observations. All three summaries use each year's longest contiguous missing run, including unions of adjacent or overlapping missing spans.

As an additional historical description, the observed archive over 1981--2025 contains 220 incomplete station-years at 31 stations; the median annual longest run is 3 days and 11.36\% have a run of at least 30 days. This historical period is supplementary rather than the primary comparison to forward masks. The calendar masks are closer to common observed gap conditions on this metric, whereas 30--90-day controlled masks probe the long-gap tail; differing sampling and repeat weights preclude a same-distribution claim.

The shared-year fraction counts station-years in which at least one other station in the same connected group also has an incomplete year (within the same replicate for trial masks). It does not require overlapping missing days and therefore does not establish synchronous daily outages or unavailable donors on the same dates.

Complete-year counts in the cohort comparison count unique complete station-years in the leave-one-year-out source cases, including the held-out year. The previous 30/7 medians counted training years: every one of the 581 stations differs by exactly one year. Correct archive medians are 31/8. Visible-donor summaries derive from leave-one-year-out cases and describe that design, rather than the forward controlled-gap experiment.
