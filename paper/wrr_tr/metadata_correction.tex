\section*{Correction to historical design metadata}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Correction to historical design metadata}
The 3,320 saved whole-archive \texttt{design.json} files are historical records and were not rewritten. Their field \texttt{annual\_model} states that ``held region and connected groups excluded.'' That statement is correct for the held test connected groups, but it is not an unconditional region exclusion. The original forward HUC17 estimator retained one other same-region training group, and NVE interval calibration retained two other same-region groups. The paper therefore distinguishes the held-test-group main analysis from the strict-region sensitivity. This correction applies to the historical metadata; it does not change any saved prediction. A correction manifest is available in \texttt{results/wrr\_adversarial\_review\_20261027/metadata\_correction/}.
