\paragraph{Fixed-budget trajectory sensitivity.} The six-station diagnostic was selected after the original results were known and is exploratory. The original 27 station-years, whole-archive masks, before-2010 daily fits, residual histories and bridge parameters remain fixed. Seeds 0, 1 and 2 change only the residual-bridge random stream; 20/100/500 are prefixes of the same 500-path sample for each seed. Seed 0 reproduces the original saved case quantiles. Each of the two region--reconstructor strata belongs to one connected group: 18 Swiss donor-ridge cases and nine Bavarian air-ridge cases. Region, reconstructor and group therefore cannot be separated by this diagnostic. Casewise endpoint changes, counts exceeding 0.05$^{\circ}$C, width and coverage are reported for every seed. A small overall median does not establish tail stability in either stratum. The 20-path comparator is a fixed computational-budget implementation. This 962 diagnostic does not re-estimate source-role offsets, and no target-subset even/odd calibration was performed. The current paper's source-role experiment is scripts/968, which re-estimates original-role offsets at each budget before scoring the same 27 targets.
