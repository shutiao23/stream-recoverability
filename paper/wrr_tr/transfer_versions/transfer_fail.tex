\subsection{New stations need local calibration of the admission threshold}
The second test used \TNewStations{} USGS stations whose water temperature had not been read by this or the earlier study when the test was designed (\TNewGroups{} independence groups in \TNewHucs{} two-digit hydrologic regions, \TNewYears{} complete station-years). Their eligibility, networks and missingness strata were fixed from the dates of approved daily means alone, and the rule, the models, the interval calibration and the code were frozen before the values were requested. The values below come from the recomputation described in Section~\ref{sec:tests}, made after the first evaluation, so this is no longer a blind test. These are new stations in regions represented in the source, not a new climate or agency.

Without screening, \TNewUnscreenedDeploy\% of D2* values failed under the stations' own gap calendars and \TNewUnscreenedMech\% under long controlled gaps. The frozen rule admits cases in increasing order of the direct failure probability while $k$ times the mean predicted probability among admitted cases stays at or below 5\%, with $k=\TNewFactor$ estimated across source regions; it uses no label of the new stations. The realised risk was \TNewRiskDeploy\% [group-bootstrap upper bound \TNewRiskDeployHigh\%] under real calendars, using \TNewRetDeploy\% of the station-years, and \TNewRiskMech\% [\TNewRiskMechHigh\%] under long gaps, using \TNewRetMech\%. The preregistered criterion of at most 7\% in both populations was not met, so the label-free threshold did not transfer to these stations. Stations with a high share of incomplete years had a realised risk of \TNewRiskDeployHighMissing\% under their own calendars, and the worst hydrologic region with at least 200 cases reached \TNewWorstRegionRisk\%.

A threshold for new stations therefore needs local labels. When the correction factor $k$ was re-estimated from masked complete years of a few local independence groups and applied to the remaining groups, \TNewLocalGroups{} groups were needed for the realised risk to stay at or below 7\% in at least 80\% of repeats. The ordering of cases, which needs no local labels, still transferred: at equal realised risk, the target-aware judgment used \TNewGainMech{} percentage points more cases than the best published rule under long gaps and \TNewGainDeploy{} points more under real calendars. The calibrated 90\% intervals covered \TNewHardCoverage\% of the truths where more than three quarters of the climatological peak window was hidden (criterion: at least 85\%; \TNewHardCoverageVerdict), and in air-layer cases with the true peak at least half hidden D2* reduced the cold bias from \TNewPOneBiasPZero{} to \TNewPOneBiasDTwo\,$^{\circ}$C, a reduction of \TNewPOneReduction\% (criterion: at least 50\%; \TNewPOneVerdict). The recovery law predicted a plug-in failure rate of \TNewLawPredicted\% from decision-time information (observed \TNewObservedFail\%).
