# Target-recovery manuscript and fourth-review revision

The fourth-review article is `review_completion_article.tex`, with
`review_completion_supporting.tex`. Its producing tables, six figures and
numerical macros come from `scripts/473_review_completion_evidence.py`.
Build them with `make wrr-review-paper`; a partial generation is marked as a
development draft. See the [execution record](../../docs/reviews/wrr_review_completion_20261024/README.md)
for actual completed runs, historical endpoint verdicts and the remaining
public-registration and new-data requirements. No unverified author identity
or permanent DOI is invented.

The current [cover letter](review_completion_cover_letter.txt),
[submission checklist](../../docs/reviews/wrr_review_completion_20261024/SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST_ZH.md)
and [reviewer-response draft](../../docs/reviews/wrr_review_completion_20261024/REVIEWER_RESPONSE_DRAFT_ZH.md)
match the fourth-review scope. Author and archive fields are for the authors to fill.

The second-revision sources below remain reproducible historical evidence.

`wrr_tr_manuscript.tex` and `wrr_tr_supporting_information.tex` use the AGU class in `../wrr/template/`.
All numbers come from `tr_claims.tex`, and all tables from `tr_table_rules.tex`, `tr_table_endpoints.tex`
and `tr_si_tables.tex`; these four files are generated by `scripts/463_tr2_evidence.py` and must not be edited by hand.
Figures 1-7 are written by `scripts/464_tr2_figures.py`; Figure S1 is exported from
`figures/wrr_tr/drawio/tr_s1_design.drawio`.

`transfer_versions/transfer_pass.tex` and `transfer_versions/transfer_fail.tex` are the two versions
of the results section on the second test. Both were first written before any water temperature
of the new stations was read. When every random seed was replaced by a plain integer, all results,
including both tests, were recomputed after their first outcomes were known; the opening paragraph
of both versions was revised to say so, and neither test is described as blind. The manuscript uses
the version named in `results/wrr_target_recovery_20261022/e10_sealed_usgs/sealed_results.json`
(`transfer_section`). The original values of all endpoints are listed in the last deviation of Text S6.

Build with `make wrr-tr-paper`.

Before submission the authors must supply names, affiliations, funding, acknowledgments and the
persistent archive DOI (all marked PENDING). They are not filled in here.
