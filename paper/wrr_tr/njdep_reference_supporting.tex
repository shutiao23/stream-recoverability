\par\medskip\noindent\textbf{New Jersey continuous-monitoring intake.} The NJDEP portal provides downloadable quality-reviewed records (\url{https://njdep.rutgers.edu/continuous/download/}). The station selector contained 287 entries, of which 156 were labeled freshwater; 86 freshwater entries lacked quality-period boundaries and remain unresolved. After discovering inconsistent water-type labels, a post-hoc check found 26 entries whose quality-period envelopes spanned at least four possible calendar years; all were labeled marine, and 24 had nonzero record counts in at least four queried years. Counts can include different variables and do not prove complete water-temperature observations. We selected 4 river/stream candidates after inspecting metadata and counts, then acquired their 2016--2019 temperature files. All 16 returned station-years lacked some reported date labels, with 37--168 missing dates per station-year. The downloaded CSV row counts agree with the provider count endpoint for all 16 station-years. An independent Arrow/Python implementation reproduced the timestamp counts without examining temperature values or model errors. The files use an EST label, but the real time basis, expected sampling cadence, nonestimated status, individual quality flags and catchment independence remain unresolved. These selected cases do not qualify as complete reference years under their reported-clock convention; this post-hoc screen does not establish the number of qualifying stations in the full portal.
