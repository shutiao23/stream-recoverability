\section{Candidate-provider, strict-region source sensitivity}
After observing the original results and LUBW source dependence, we refitted all 13 whole-archive source models on seven candidate providers with recorded reuse paths. Role assignments and bootstrap draws were recomputed using the filtered data, and every group occurring in the target region was excluded from source roles. An independent role recount found no positive-weight target-region source case or post-2009 forward source case. Original target masks and daily features were reused. All 3,320 archive settings, masks and the unchanged plug-in baselines were checked. Removing providers, changing source assignments and strict regional exclusion jointly change the analysis; the result is not a causal attribution to one of them. The filtered inputs remain processed study data, not provider-native raw records. This after-the-fact sensitivity neither establishes legal redistribution rights nor independent climate transfer, and it does not replace the original analysis.
